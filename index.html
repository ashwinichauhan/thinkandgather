CONVERSATION · IDEAS · CURIOSITY

We need to talk
about something.

Most of us spend enough time scrolling. Think & Gather is an excuse to do something else for a few hours.

Show up. Meet someone new. Have a conversation you didn't expect.

We host a different kind of gathering each month. Debates, book clubs, murder mysteries, movie nights, potlucks, workshops — whatever gives us a reason to get together and use our brains a little. Some nights you'll learn something. Some nights you'll change your mind. Hopefully, every night you'll meet someone worth knowing.

A NOTE ON THE ROOM — 01
Phones down.
Go deeper.

Think & Gather is a break from surface-level conversation. For a couple of hours, the usual “what do you do?” can give way to what you believe, what you’re curious about, what you’ve changed your mind on, and what you’re still trying to understand.

Less small talk. More to talk about.
